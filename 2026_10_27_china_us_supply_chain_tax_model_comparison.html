Strategic Recommendation & Decision Logic

Final executive evaluation for supply chain expansion and leadership alignment.

Why Option A (HK Principal) is Superior for US DTC Expansion

For high-velocity, high-margin direct-to-consumer (DTC) or Amazon/marketplace operations, Option A provides maximum capital efficiency, offshore profit flexibility, and minimal regulatory overhead in mainland China.

Profit Shielding

Restricts China’s 25% CIT solely to the cost-plus service fee (3%–10% on OpEx). Net trading margins remain offshore in Hong Kong, subject to HK's 16.5% rate or 0% under Foreign Source Income Exemption (FSIE).

Cash Flow Optimization

Completely eliminates China domestic 13% input VAT payments. Bypasses 30–90 day Export VAT Refund delays, unlocking working capital for marketing and inventory scalability.

Section 321 T86 Synergies

Direct factory dispatch from China consolidation hubs (Dongguan) under HK title allows seamless Section 321 de minimis import clearance ($800/day duty-free) into the US.

Transfer Pricing Safety

Cost-plus service arrangements (3%–10%) are standard and widely accepted by State Taxation Administration (STA) in China for administrative and QC support WFOEs.

When to Consider Option B

While Option B creates higher tax leakage, it may be required under specific factory relationship parameters:

  • Factories without Export Licenses: Small factories unable to clear China export customs directly require a domestic buyer.
  • Significant RMB Discounts (>15%): If suppliers offer domestic RMB pricing significantly lower than USD export pricing, offsetting CIT leakage.
  • Onshore China Sales Strategy: If the China entity also sells directly to domestic Chinese consumers or marketplaces (Tmall/JD).
Executive Takeaway: Unless domestic suppliers offer massive RMB price concessions, Option A remains the strategic default.
